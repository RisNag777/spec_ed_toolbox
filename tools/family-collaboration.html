Family & Community Collaboration

Addressing disproportionality requires dismantling institutional power dynamics and partnering authentically with families from historically marginalized communities during the referral and IEP process.

  • Connect with families early during Tier 1 and Tier 2 MTSS/RtI interventions to share student strengths and discuss general education supports well before any evaluation is suggested.
  • Provide all evaluation notices, meeting agendas, and progress monitoring reports in the family's primary language using certified, professional interpreters.
  • Begin referral and IEP meetings by inviting parents to share their child's home strengths, cultural knowledge, and personal aspirations. Address historical stigmas around special education openly, ensuring family priorities directly shape educational goals

Solutions

  • Least Restrictive Environment (LRE)
  • Response to Intervention (RtI)
  • Teacher/Assessment Bias Interruption
