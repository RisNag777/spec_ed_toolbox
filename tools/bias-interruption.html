Teacher/Assessment Bias Interruption

Unexamined teacher expectations and culturally biased assessments can directly accelerate racial disparities in subjective disability categories. Assessors must check their biases to prevent diagnosing cultural variations as cognitive deficits. Educators should complete this self-reflection checklist prior to drafting referral narratives:

  • Am I evaluating this student’s academic performance or behavioral style through a subjective lens?
  • Are the diagnostic tools free from cultural and linguistic bias, and do they accurately measure student capability?
  • Have I maintained high academic standards and provided robust instructional scaffolding, or am I operating under implicit assumptions that lower standards for racially or culturally diverse students?
